All three are U.S. industrial registrants with consolidated U.S. GAAP financial statements in USD. They form a useful test cohort for common financial definitions. They span different industrial businesses, so peer comparisons should be descriptive and should show the actual reporting dates and business scope.

The EDGAR backfill confirms 23 base reports each for ITW and DOV, 20 for ROK, and one ITW amendment. Each full fiscal year contributes three 10-Qs and one 10-K. The backfill also captured the preceding fiscal year for each issuer to support opening trailing-twelve-month calculations. [Backfill and accession inventory](sec_backfill.md) For ROK, 31 December 2025 is FY2026 Q1. September 2026 is Q3 for ITW and DOV and annual year-end for ROK. Compare compatible September balance-sheet balances on the same date. Align the duration before comparing flow metrics, such as by using four validated fiscal quarters.

## Metric coverage in the reviewed filings

The table records disclosure in the primary statements or notes. A disclosed line can still require a tag mapping or an adjustment before it is eligible for cross-company comparison.

| Required input | ITW | DOV | ROK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash and equivalents | Balance-sheet cash and equivalents. | Balance-sheet cash and cash equivalents. | Balance-sheet cash and cash equivalents. Check restricted-cash disclosures separately. |
| Working capital and liquidity ratios | Current assets and current liabilities. | Current assets and current liabilities. Retain held-for-sale classifications in the periods where reported. | Current assets and current liabilities. |
| Debt and net debt | Short-term debt and long-term debt. Commercial paper is already part of short-term debt in the latest reviewed quarter. | Use reported current borrowings and noncurrent debt without re-adding components. Some periods combine short-term borrowings and current maturities. Latest reviewed quarter presents the current portion of long-term debt. | Separately disclosed short-term debt, current portion of long-term debt, and noncurrent long-term debt. The annual filing states that current and noncurrent finance leases are included in the relevant debt lines. Use the lease note where it supports a same-date adjustment. Where that is unverified, label the combined basis and exclude incompatible debt rankings. |
| Operating cash flow | Reported cash from operating activities. Interim statements provide fiscal year-to-date intervals. | Operating cash flow is available. The reviewed filings separate discontinued operations, so record continuing and discontinued scope explicitly before deriving FCF or growth. | Operating cash flow is available. Interim intervals start on 1 October. Avoid comparing a nine-month statement to a full-year statement as if they covered the same duration. |
| Cash capital expenditure and FCF | Additions to plant and equipment. | Additions to property, plant and equipment. Match the operating cash-flow scope. | Capital expenditures in the cash-flow statement. Use the cash amount rather than accrued capex in the notes. |
| Revenue and net income for ratios | Both disclosed. Flag the inventory-method change when interpreting the trend. | Both disclosed. Match continuing versus total earnings and cash-flow bases before calculating margin or cash conversion. | Both disclosed. Distinguish consolidated net income from income attributable to the parent when matching the CFO numerator. |

## Required comparability flags

- **ITW, FY2024 Q1 inventory accounting change.** Certain U.S. businesses changed from LIFO to FIFO. The filing reports a cumulative effect in Q1. Preserve the original reports, flag the break when comparing working capital and earnings-based metrics, and check if a comparative amount actually changed before calling it a revision. [FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/49826/000004982625000007/itw-20241231.htm)
- **DOV, FY2024 Environmental Solutions Group disposition.** The Q3 filing presents the business as held for sale and discontinued. The December 10-K documents the October sale. Check whether Q2 and Q3 year-to-date flows share the same reporting basis before deriving Q3. If a compatible earlier operand is unavailable, suppress that calculation. Use the separate continuing and discontinued data as a reporting-scope case, with the original and later comparative filings linked. [Q2 2024 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29905/000002990524000035/dov-20240630.htm), [Q3 2024 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29905/000002990524000045/dov-20240930.htm), [FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29905/000002990525000006/dov-20241231.htm)
- **ROK, finance leases and Sensia.** Verify the lease adjustment in every affected debt period. The FY2026 annual filing also records the Sensia joint venture dissolution and related changes to consolidation. Flag comparisons that span the change and preserve the period's reported cash flow and business scope. [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1024478/000102447826000048/rok-20260930.htm), [Sensia disclosure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1024478/000102447826000048/R11.htm)
